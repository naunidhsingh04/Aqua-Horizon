====================================================================================================
PART 5 - KNOWN EXCEPTIONS (stated openly)
====================================================================================================
* ml/test_10fold_irs.py and ml/test_satellite_learning.py now read the real district_year_dataset.csv;
  test_10fold_irs.py applies SMOTE inside each training fold only. No benchmark uses a pre-balanced dataset.
* The image pipeline picks its best epoch using the test fold's accuracy, so image scores are slightly optimistic.
* ml/train_kfold_hybrid_models.py uses no resampling: it balances with a class-weighted loss instead.
